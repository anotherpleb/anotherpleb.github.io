Nature Hazard Awareness

A broad, region-by-region heads-up on the kinds of wildlife, plants, and terrain hazards that exist in different parts of the world — for people whose whole life has been indoors, pavement, and climate control, and who don't know what they don't know.

Deliberately broad, not exhaustive. This is general awareness, not a field guide, an identification tool, or safety instruction — categories only, no species-level detail. Every region has exceptions and local specifics this can't capture. Before spending real time outdoors somewhere unfamiliar, get local, current information (park service, ranger station, local guide) — and never eat a wild plant, berry, or mushroom based on anything here.
